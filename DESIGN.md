# Bijou design

Register: product. A character atelier with a quiet toy-box interface.

## Palette and scene

Warm charcoal surrounds an ivory stage. Rose marks selection and primary actions;
gold and mint appear in the character art. UI colors use OKLCH in `css/style.css`.
The shared header receives its palette through its public tokens, with no edits to
the vendored kit. Other selected header themes keep their own colors.

## Typography and layout

Use the existing system font for controls, navigation and instructions. The name
on the stage uses Georgia as a small character-title detail. Keep the full figure
visible next to the dressing panel on desktop. Stack stage and controls on narrow
screens, reserving space below the illustration for touch actions.

## Character drawing

The live figure uses a continuous skinned 3D surface, an orthographic camera,
coordinated 3D anime shading, with physical and 2D finishes available. Hime with the oval face is the
default. Face, hair, garment fit and proportions stay independently
editable. Keep the original SVG renderer as a fallback, following `docs/RIG.md`.
The model's original clothes, ribbon and tied back hair are removed from the asset;
wardrobe items and hair additions are generated by Bijou.

Body shaping uses bounded regional morphs and a matching skeleton bind pose.
Skin and garment shadows follow the same deformation. Arm collisions use simple
body volumes with clothing clearance. Keep soft motion subtle by default and
respect reduced motion. Avoid hiding mesh problems behind heavy outlines.

## Interactions

Signature looks change hair and outfit while preserving the visitor's body, name,
room and saved looks. All preset items are free. Individual items remain removable
and interchangeable under the existing slot rules. Keep the base slip, animated
expressions, pointer reactions, reduced motion, persistence and companion modes.

Use visible focus rings, descriptive button labels and `aria-pressed` for selection.
Locked items stay visible enough to preview their color and silhouette.

The Body tab provides presets, region selection, equivalent width/depth sliders,
undo/redo, and motion controls. Shape mode temporarily holds a neutral pose and
separates sculpting gestures from pats and picking up. Front, profile and back
views are explicit. One drag or pinch is one undo step; cancellation restores the
shape from before the gesture. Saved looks include proportions and motion settings.
Archetypes offer proportions and movement starting points while preserving face
and outfit. Region, hand and face inspection make fine adjustments visible.
Keep feature progress and unfinished visual work in `docs/COMPANION-ROADMAP.md`.
