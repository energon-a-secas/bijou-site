# Bijou: product one-pager

**What it is:** Dress, pose and keep an anime doll on your desktop

**Who it is for:** someone who spends long stretches at a computer and wants a small, cute companion on screen: a dress-up doll they can style, unlock clothes for, and keep beside their work, without installing anything.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits). It is closer to a toy than a tool: the doll is the interface, the panel is the toy box.

**The scene:** a laptop on a desk in the evening, a work window open, and Bijou popped out into a small floating window in the corner, blinking and swaying, dozing off when ignored and waking up with a pat. A warm charcoal and rose shell frames an ivory character atelier so the doll is the brightest thing on screen.

**Art direction:** adult anime figure proportions with long legs, softly tapered limbs, rounded volume and the finish of a 3D figurine. Hime hair and the existing oval face are the default; copper bob, rose hair and other variants remain available. Sea-glass bikini replaces the Crimson ribbon starting ensemble. Keep the figure rigged and all clothing editable; signature looks are combinations of wardrobe items. Body anatomy, natural posture and detailed customization take priority over additional outfits. Track the evolving requests and research in [the companion roadmap](docs/COMPANION-ROADMAP.md).

**What done looks like:** she is wearing an outfit the visitor chose, she has reacted to them at least once, and they have earned enough hearts to look forward to the next unlock.

**Body customization:** make proportions meaningfully editable, with rounded and
slender starting points, fitting clothes, mouse/touch shaping and precise sliders.
Prioritize natural poses and collision clearance before secondary motion. Bounce
is adjustable, optional, and bounded; shaping pauses it for predictable editing.
Regional selection should zoom to the detail, allow orbiting and return to normal
companion behavior on exit. Cup volume is independent of torso depth; extra
regional fullness is independent of clothing fit. Generic clothing patterns need
editable cuts, fabric and fitted/oversized options. The default 3D anime finish
keeps face, body and hair coherent; cel-animation and physical finishes are choices.
