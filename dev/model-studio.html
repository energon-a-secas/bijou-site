About this model

These are adult anime character studies with editable proportions and face treatments. Game heroine takes inspiration from Tifa’s Remake appearance; Fantasy realism is an experimental surface and face treatment.

Hime has a clean anime face, shaped hair and a softer standing pose. The original tank and shorts remain beneath fitted layers; shorter cuts still need an underlying body asset. Detailed anatomy, garment construction and strand grooming remain unfinished.

Your draft saves separately for each model. Look files retain all settings. Downloads preserve edited geometry, colors, roughness, clothing, hair, skeleton and the source walking clip. Manual gaze and expression export. Automatic motion, studio light and cel shading stay in the preview.
