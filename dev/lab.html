Physics

Body bounce runs from still (0) to a modest cap (1) that is never looser than the model authored. Presets, variation and touch never loosen the body; hair, skirts and ribbons carry the jiggle. Carry swing is how much of being carried and tossed the springs feel.

Fine tuning

Variation gives each strand its own stiffness and drag, so strands never swing in lockstep; the body is never varied. Hair cone is how far each hair joint may swing from rest (180 is free); ribbons keep their own 60 degree cone. Squash and lean are the whole-body springs, in hertz and damping ratio (1 never overshoots).
