# CLAUDE.md: Bijou

An animated anime dress-up doll and desktop companion with a skinned 3D renderer and an SVG fallback. Drag clothes onto her, reshape and restyle her, keep her company, and pop her out into an always-on-top window. **Read [`docs/RIG.md`](docs/RIG.md) before touching any drawing**: it is the contract every body, face, hair and garment function speaks.

**Live:** bijou.neorgon.com · **Port:** 8897

## Run

```bash
make serve
```

Then open http://localhost:8897. It must be served over HTTP: the app is ES modules, and `file://` blocks them.

## Architecture

The live renderer is `avatar.js`. The `avatar-*` modules own model preparation,
arm IK/collisions, materials, fitted and generated garments, morphs, hair and face.
`body-shape.js` supplies the shared regional field; `body-editor.js` owns gestures
and history. `pose-reaches.js` adds 3D targets to the existing pose language.
`soft-spring.js` is a pure fixed-step solver. The local engine bundle in
`js/vendor/` is generated by `dev/vendor-avatar.sh`, not hand edited.
`avatar-surface.js` refines body topology; `avatar-skinning.js` supplies shared
dual-quaternion skinning. `hair-chain.js` and `avatar-hair-motion.js` own generated
hair motion. `archetypes.js` supplies editable starting bodies and mannerisms.
`avatar-fit.js` fits clothing after body morphing; `garment-templates.js` validates
the pattern settings edited by `clothing-editor.js`. `avatar-finish.js` coordinates
anime shading; `avatar-support.js` and `avatar-correctives.js` own joint/support
corrections. User-facing Basics contains the free editable clothing patterns.
Keep requests and research current in `docs/COMPANION-ROADMAP.md`; distinguish
implemented controls from unfinished visual polish and future simulation work.
`dev/model-studio.html` edits imported Meshy candidates through `model-shape.js`
and `model-sculpt.js`; `model-appearance.js` owns adult face treatments,
`model-wardrobe.js` owns fitted garment layers, and `model-normals.js` keeps
extrusion directions continuous at UV seams. `model-limbs.js` supplies independent
arm lengths. `model-hair*` owns sculpted locks, source-fringe tint/export and guide
motion; `model-gaze.js`/`model-eyelids.js` own gaze, blinks and head following.
`model-persona.js` validates their saved settings. `model-finish.js` is studio-only
figurine/physical shading. `model-face-details.js` supplies bounded facial fields;
`model-style.js` validates skin/iris/makeup and studio lighting; `model-portraits.js`
supplies adult starts/seeded variations. `model-surface-color.js` shares preview
and UV-space export color masks with `model-atlas-bake.js`. `model-smooth-normals.js`
repairs remesh shading without changing positions.
`model-anime-head.js` replaces Hime's damaged fused head with the existing CC0
anime face, preserving UV/alpha edges and authored blink deltas. It preserves
shoulders when clipping the old neck and removes disconnected hair fragments.
Hime/Oval remains the fresh default, with balanced Figurine body proportions;
saved drafts retain their body settings. The original Game heroine head is a
separate choice/draft. The cleaned body does not use atlas hair visibility masks.
Fitted clothing hides fully covered base triangles; fitting normals must always
use the complete immutable source index, never that visibility index.
`model-face-normals.js` fits local facial shading directions, excluding hair and
opposing surfaces. Tangents regenerate after shaping and follow gaze derivatives.
Eye surface restoration, source hair finish and diffuse falloff use the shared
validated style schema and existing v4 migrations. Version 4 files retain all five
editors and lighting, and accept version 1/2/3 recipes. It uses +Z-front metres and its own drafts, not VRM raw
space or companion looks. `dev/model-studio-check.cjs` validates edits and GLB
export/reload. The source inspector remains `dev/asset-study.html`; raw API
responses and credentials must stay outside the served repo.
`dev/prepare-human-study.py` and `human-study-builder.js` prepare the pinned CC0
adult anatomical source, its 163-joint skeleton, separate eyes, opaque base
clothing and relaxed rest pose. The user rejected its visual result; it is
withdrawn from the picker and public query links. Its assets/drafts are preserved.
Internal development checks use `modelStudio.selectCandidate('human')` after
loading the studio. Metadata calibrates face/eye fields and
bypasses remesh normal repair. `model-human-eyelids.js` supplies curved blink
covers; independent eyes rotate on their own joints. `model-fabric-envelope.js`
bridges small contours beneath its upper garments, regenerating their normals.
`node dev/model-human-check.cjs` validates body/face edits, actual eye rotation,
blink covers, coverage, saved recipes, history, export/reload and resource cycles.
Run `node --test dev/model-shape.test.mjs dev/model-persona.test.mjs dev/model-details.test.mjs` and
`node dev/model-anime-check.cjs` for the corrective head, iris/lid motion, body
locality, base coverage, transparent GLB edges, saved drafts and resource cycles.
The older source-specific regression helpers remain available. Run
`node dev/model-persona-check.cjs` for limb, eye, expression, tracking, hair and
whole-look export checks. `node dev/model-details-check.cjs` checks each facial
detail on both sources, skin/iris/roughness export pixels, regional cameras,
lighting history, portrait variations, v3 migration and mobile/reduced motion.
See `docs/CHARACTER-CREATOR.md` for the Aion-inspired scope and art requirements.
Hair motion and automatic facial behavior are preview
effects; manual gaze/expression and hair rest geometry export.

## Data

One Persist Kit store, `bijou-site:save` (version 1): name, body, head, outfit, per-pattern `garments`, `renderStyle`, rendering quality, unlocked/seen items, hearts, milestones, stats, daily earnings, room, and up to eight saved looks. Looks include tailoring and finish. `state.js` validates every stored field against its schema; older saves receive neutral/default values. The active style wish is runtime only.

## Conventions

- Visual checks: `dev/sheet.html` renders a grid of static dolls from the real modules. `?m=poses`, `?m=items&slot=dress`, `?m=heads`, `?m=bodies`; `&c=6&h=290` sets columns and cell height; `&zoom=x,y,w,h` crops every doll to a viewBox. Check every new garment and pose there before the live page.
- Live 3D checks: `dev/model.html?look=seaglass&pose=stretch`; `window.study` exposes builds, poses, stepping and diagnostics. `&linear=1` enables source linear skinning for comparison. Run `node --test dev/avatar-math.test.mjs` after collision, morph or spring changes. Run `node dev/avatar-browser.cjs` for browser checks (Playwright and the local server are required).
- Zero build step. Plain ES modules loaded by `js/app.js`.
- Run `node --test dev/avatar-foundation.test.mjs` for support IK, corrective bounds, surface probes, chest controls and tailoring validation. `node dev/avatar-customization.cjs` checks the new editor controls, saved looks and floor/seat support in the browser.
- Header and footer come from the shared kits. Do not add site-local `.neo-footer` or `.header-bar` CSS.
- Keep owned JS modules under ~500 lines. Generated vendor bundles are excluded.

## Gotchas

- **3D rest space faces -Z.** The VRM scene rotates it to face the camera. Morphs, garment coordinates, IK targets and colliders use raw rest space. Do not mix camera coordinates into these calculations.
- **Rebind only in rest pose.** `avatar-morph.js` restores the bone hierarchy before calculating bind inverses. Every shape change updates body, garments and collider dimensions together. Keep `bjRest` unchanged for garment masks.
- **Shape mode owns its gestures.** Play interactions return early while editing. Gesture cancellation restores the preceding body; slider and pointer edits use the same validated values.
- **Fit after shaping.** Garment allowance is applied to the shaped body, never passed through body-region classification. Keep anatomical and garment ease controls separate. Fitted and generated garments both need a meaningful result for every exposed tailoring control.
- **Source springs are retired.** Retained bangs follow the head; generated hair guides own motion. Re-enabling the source spring manager causes fringe recoil on outfit rebuilds and breaks reduced-motion behavior.
- The drawing notes below apply to the **SVG fallback**.

- **No gradient fills on limb pieces.** A per-piece `objectBoundingBox` gradient ends dark at one piece's edge and starts light at the next, so every elbow and knee seams. Skin and multi-piece garments use `cel()` with `dy: 0` (a sideways-only shadow crescent); single-piece shapes may shift diagonally.
- **IK bend flips above the shoulder.** `bend: 'out'` adds the elbow angle to the target direction, which wraps past 180 degrees for a hand raised above the shoulder and swings the elbow across her face. The peace pose uses `'in'` for that reason; check any new raised-hand pose on the dev sheet.
- **Pieces are flat, not nested.** Each piece gets its bone's world matrix; depth is only `z`. An arm posed behind her (`band: 'back'`) or in front of her face (`'front'`) shifts all of that arm's pieces at once. `fixed: true` opts a piece out (the parasol canopy).
- **The head is drawn large and scaled.** Head art is canonical (chin at y 244) and scaled by `HEAD_SCALE` 0.76 about the chin. Head-bone coordinates in `wear-acc.js` are canonical, not body coordinates.
- **The base slip is not optional.** `outfitPieces` draws it whenever no dress or swimwear is on and the slot it would cover is empty. Never add a control that removes it.
- **Held items stay upright.** Pieces with `upright` are placed at the palm's world position with no rotation (and scaled 1.3), so a cup never tips when the arm moves.
- **The stage moves documents.** Pop out moves `#stage` into the Picture-in-Picture window and hands the animation loop that window's `requestAnimationFrame`. Listeners belong on the stage, never on `window` or `document`; `dnd.js` refuses drops while the stage is away.
- **Wishes must be grantable.** She only wishes for a theme the unlocked wardrobe can already satisfy; on day one every rain item is locked.
- **Clip ids are global.** `cel()` mints a fresh clip id per shape, so thumbnails and mini dolls never collide with the stage doll; cached thumbnail strings may repeat an id, which is harmless because the geometry is identical.
