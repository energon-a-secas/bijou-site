        Keep Bijou on your desktop

        A floating window could not be opened here. Two ways to keep her around:

        • In Chrome or Edge, the Pop out button opens a small window that stays on top of everything else.
        • In Safari on a Mac, choose File, then Add to Dock. Bijou opens in her own window, and Companion view below fits her to it.
